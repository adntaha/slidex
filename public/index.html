Talk. The slides write themselves.

SlideX listens while you speak and builds the deck as you go, with titles, bullets, and a picture for every slide. You never have to turn your back on the room.

Start presenting

Live from speech

The deck follows the talk: a new subject opens a new slide, and the slide being written stays on screen.

Illustrated as it goes

Each slide is illustrated from Wikimedia Commons, credited on the slide, and never repeats a picture already on the deck.

Saved when you reset

Pressing reset clears the deck and writes the finished talk to a single PDF in the exports folder.
